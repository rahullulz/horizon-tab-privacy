# Horizon Tab — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: June 2026_

Horizon Tab respects your privacy. This policy explains exactly what the
extension does with data. In short: your personal content stays on your device,
and we never sell or share your data.

## What we store (on your device)
- **Your settings** — clock style, toggles, wallpaper category, music mood,
  theme, world-clock cities, and feature preferences. Saved using Chrome's
  storage, including Chrome Sync if you are signed into Chrome.
- **Your notes, to-do tasks, and daily focus** — stored locally in your browser.
- **Your name** (optional) — if you set a greeting name, it is stored locally to
  personalize your greeting. It is never sent anywhere.
- **Custom wallpaper** (Pro) — if you upload one, it is stored locally and never
  leaves your device.
- **Focus session history** — a local count of completed focus sessions, stored
  on your device to show your streak.
- **Pro license key** (if you buy Pro) — stored so the extension remembers you
  are a Pro user.

## Location (for weather)
- If you enable the Weather feature, the extension asks your browser for your
  approximate location, with your permission.
- Your coordinates are sent to a weather provider (Open-Meteo) to retrieve local
  conditions, and to a location-name provider (BigDataCloud) to show your city
  name.
- Your location is cached on your device and is used only to display weather. We
  do not store your location on any server we operate, and we do not track or log
  your movements. You can turn the Weather feature off at any time.

## What we send to our server
- **Wallpapers:** the extension asks our server for an image in your chosen
  category. The request contains only the category name (e.g. "nature").
- **Music:** the extension asks our server for a playlist in your chosen mood
  (e.g. "focus") and streams audio through our server. No personal data is sent.
- **Pro activation:** when you activate a Pro key, the key is sent to our server
  once to verify it.

## Third-party services we contact
- **Open-Meteo** — weather data (only if Weather is enabled).
- **BigDataCloud** — converts coordinates to a city name (only if Weather is
  enabled).
- **Wikimedia / Wikipedia** — provides the optional "On This Day" history fact
  (only if that feature is enabled). No personal data is sent.
- **Google favicons** — loads small site icons for your shortcuts.
- **Pexels & Pixabay** — wallpaper images, served via our server.
- **Jamendo** — Creative Commons music, streamed via our server.

We only contact these services to provide the feature you enabled, and we send
the minimum needed (e.g. a category name, a mood, or — for weather only — your
coordinates).

## What we do NOT do
- We do not collect, sell, rent, or share your personal data.
- We do not track your browsing history.
- We do not inject ads or change your search engine.
- Your notes, tasks, name, focus history, and custom wallpapers are never
  transmitted to any server we operate.

## Your control
Every data-using feature (Weather, World Clocks, On This Day, music, wallpapers)
can be turned off in the extension's settings. Disabling a feature stops the
related network requests.

## Changes
We may update this policy as the extension evolves. The "last updated" date at
the top reflects the latest version.
